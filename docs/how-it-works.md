# How It Works

A short guide to tokens, Minis, wild Minis, the Arcade and its games on HomeCraft. In game, open
it with `/hcm guide`, from the Arcade, or from the **Guide** site on any PC.

## Tokens

Tokens are the Arcade's coins. You can't buy them with money, and they never turn into money.
You earn them by playing:

| How | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Log in every day** | Your streak pays more each day in a row: 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, then 5 a day. It counts in the Games world too. |
| **Play** | A token for every hour you play, in the Games world too. |
| **Quests** | Three daily jobs and two weekly ones, different for everyone, like "Catch 8 fish" or "Visit 3 different biomes". |
| **Achievements** | Big moments pay once, like your first Mini or a 7-day streak. |
| **Wild Minis** | Catching one counts toward quests and achievements. |
| **Card trade-in** | Swap spare Cards for a few tokens at the Prize Counter. |

Spend them in the **Arcade** (`/hcm arcade`):

- **Crates.** Pay tokens and get a surprise: a boost, a Mini Radar, fireworks, a 1-day trail… or, rarely, a Card for any Mini. Hats are never in a crate: you buy them.
- **Scratch Ticket.** 10 tokens. Scratch three squares; three the same wins. The jackpot grows with every ticket anyone buys.
- **Prize Counter.** Pick exactly what you want, at a fixed price:
  - Boosts: speed, mining, night vision, water breathing, lucky fishing, Bouncy Boots (jump high) and Floaty Feather (float down). **Make it Day!** turns night into day in your world, and **Sunny Day** stops the rain for 10 minutes. You can't buy them when it's already day or not raining, so you never pay for nothing.
  - Hunt gear: a Mini Radar and a Mini Lure (when wild Minis are on).
  - Fireworks & Trails (one row of fireworks, then a row of trails): fireworks (the **Firework Show**, and the **Grand Firework Show**: 15 seconds with a big finale. One grand show at a time: wait for it to finish before the next. A grand show is for home, not the games: it stops if you join a game, and a tap on the Arcade Machine, a game's sign or a Mini doesn't use it up), trails for a month, and **Party Time!**: 10 seconds of fireworks around everyone who's playing.
    - The **Mega Firework Show** (75 tokens): a whole minute of fireworks from your **Firework Launchers**, for a special day. Buy three launchers (5 tokens each, yours to keep), put them in a row a few steps apart with open sky above, stand near them (within 32 blocks) facing them, and use the show: tap a launcher, the ground or the air. Each launcher has its own colours, and it ends with gold from all three. It keeps going if you walk away. With one or two launchers it fires from those; with none nearby, or if they're in someone's show already, it stays in your bag. It counts as your one long show, like the grand one, and not in the games.
    - Each player can have 15 launchers set up at once. Only you (or an admin) can pick yours up, and you can use anyone's that are near you. A tap on a launcher says whose it is.
    - Bigger shows: the **Super Firework Show** (100 tokens, 4 launchers), the **Ultra Firework Show** (125, 5 launchers) and the **Ultimate Firework Show** (200, 10 launchers, a minute and a half, for special events). Each launcher has its own colours. With fewer launchers they fire the same show from the ones you have.
    - The **Firework Remote** (10 tokens, yours to keep): tap your launchers with it in the order you want them to fire. Tap one again to take it off. Up to 10. Then a show fires from those, wherever they are, as long as someone is near them (and you see the rockets from about 30-40 blocks, so link the ones near where you'll watch). With nothing linked, a show uses the launchers nearest you, as before.
    - While you hold the Remote (in either hand), each linked launcher that can fire right now has its number floating over it ("#3"). Tap the ground with the Remote for **My Show**: your launchers, numbered and coloured, how far away and which way, where exactly and whose, "not loaded" for one too far from everyone to fire, a **Show Map** (a real map with a flag for each launcher; on Bedrock the flags show their colours, not their numbers) and Clear all. A dropped Show Map disappears after a few minutes like any dropped item: get it again from My Show, for free.
    - **Holiday shows:** on Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's, St Patrick's and Independence Day, the long shows come in that holiday's colours, with its own shape mixed in (creeper faces for Halloween!). The 3-second Firework Show stays as it is.
    - Halloween collectors: the Autumn Leaves 2026 trail (October and November). Yours for good. Tap one to buy it for yourself, or tap Give a present to buy one for someone who's online.
    - Christmas collectors: the Twinkle 2026 trail (1 December to 6 January). Yours for good, and it can be a present too.
  - **Looks:** hats and caps for 90 days (the Creeper Hat, the Dragon Hat and four colourful caps), golf balls you keep, and the collector caps: the Pumpkin Cap 2026 (October) and the Santa Cap 2026 (December), yours for good.
  - Perks: an extra home, `/hat`.
  - Trophies: a numbered Arcade Trophy (each one costs a bit more), a trophy ladder from Bronze to Legend, and a Trophy of the Month.
  - Minis: a Rare Card once a week, filament, a Display Case.
  - **Keepers:** the Legend Crown, three keeper trails and two keeper hats. Yours for good.
- **Card Packs.** Some packs can be bought with tokens as well as dollars.
- The Arcade has one row for each thing you do: **Play** (while the games are on), the **Prize Counter**,
  **Luck** (the crates, the Scratch Ticket, Card Packs, Take a break) and **You** (quests, achievements, My
  Looks, this guide).

Your **Wallet** (top of the Arcade) shows your tokens, your streak, when your next playtime token
comes, and your last ten token changes.

Arcade prizes can't be sold for money. Shops, Pallets, vending machines, the Auction House and
Courier runs all say no. In the trade window they can be swapped for other things, but never for
money.

Prizes stay with you when you die. Carry them loose, not inside a bundle or a shulker box: a prize
inside one drops with it.

A hat or trophy you place stays put: pistons, water, lava, TNT and withers can't move it, and only
you (or an admin) can break it. It goes back in your bag (or your Mailbox, if your bag is full).
**Tap a placed Arcade Trophy and the Arcade opens** (crouch to build next to it instead).

## Looks that run out, and buying again

Hats, caps and trails you wear are **yours for a while**: hats and caps for 90 days, trails for 30.
Everything else stays yours for good: trophies, golf balls, `/hat`, extra homes, the collectors and
the Keepers.

- A look's name says how long it's yours: "Pink Cap - until 18 Jan". It ends at midnight.
- A week before it ends, and the day before, a message on your screen says so (on Bedrock it waits
  until you close a menu or an Arcade game, so you see it). When it ends, it "went back to the shop".
  A crate's 1-day trail just says so in chat.
- **Buy it again** from a week before it ends until a month after, for the same price. It never buys
  itself again, so nothing is ever spent without you.
- Your hat stays with you: it can't be dropped, put in a chest or traded (your ender chest is fine).
  Lost it? My Looks makes it again for free.
- Someone can buy you a look as a present (Give a present). When it's a week from ending, they hear
  about it too (if you weren't on that week, they hear when you next are).
- A rented cap is a dyed leather cap and the mob hats are the game's own heads, so anyone could craft
  the same look. What the tokens buy is that yours can't be lost and comes back free.

## My Looks

Open **My Looks** from your Wallet, or type `/hcm looks` (or `/hcm trail`).

- **Tap a hat** to put it on. **Tap a trail** to switch it on. Only one trail shows at a time, and only
  while you walk.
- The top row shows what's on your head (tap to take it off) and your trail (tap to switch it off, tap
  again to switch it back on).
- Each look says how long it's yours. A small number on it means only a few days are left.
- Your collector caps are there too, with where each one is.
- **"Other people's trails and parties"** (bottom row): tap to hide other people's trails, sparkles
  and Party Time fireworks, just for you. Your own trail always shows to you.

## The Keepers

The Keepers are the biggest things on the Prize Counter, and they are yours for good:

| Keeper | Tokens | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Legend Crown** | 5,000 | A gold crown with your number on it, and its own Royal Trail. |
| **Aurora Trail** | 3,000 | Green and purple lights float up around you. |
| **Stardust Trail** | 3,000 | Golden sparkles drift down behind you. |
| **Frost Trail** | 3,000 | Icy sparkles and snow at your feet. |
| **Wizard Hat** | 2,500 | A midnight hat with golden swirls. A little magic floats above it. |
| **Knight's Helm** | 2,500 | A shining knight's helmet, just for looks. A tiny flame on its crest. |

Only one of each, and only you can wear yours. A Keeper can't be given as a present, traded or
dropped. A worn Keeper hat shows a small sparkle of its own above your head as you walk: nobody can
craft that.

## Trading

Type `/trade <name>`, or crouch and right-click someone with an empty hand. On Bedrock, a "Trade?"
screen asks if you want to: tap Yes. The PC's Trade site works too.

1. **Put things on the table:** tap them in your bag. On Bedrock, tap something on the table to take
   half of it back.
2. **Money** can go in too, but never with an Arcade prize on the table. Tokens never.
3. **Both press Ready.** If anything changes, both have to press it again. Giving something away for
   nothing needs a second tap: "Give it away? Tap again".
4. **3, 2, 1, traded!** Nothing leaves your bag until then. If something you get doesn't fit, it's in
   your Mailbox.

If the server stops in the middle of the swap, nothing is lost: an admin checks the trade and puts it
right. If it has to be undone, what you gave comes back to your Mailbox ("Back from your trade with
..."), and a Mini you gave is yours again too. Nothing is taken out of your bag on its own, and a trade
that finished stays finished: what it put in your Mailbox stays there. Money isn't paid back that way:
an admin checks it. A trade that couldn't quite finish while the server kept
running says "will reach your Mailbox once the trade is checked": that happens after the server's next
restart, once an admin has looked. If money you put on the table couldn't reach the other player, you
are told it went back to you (or, if even that failed, to ask an admin, with the trade's number).

Close the window and nothing happens: everything is still yours. Game items, Keepers, rented looks,
Courier packages and the Trick-or-Treat Bag can't be traded. Minis change owner when they're traded.
Type `/trade off` (or use the Trade site's tile) to stop people asking you.

## Candy apples (until 31 Oct)

Every hour in October (every half hour on Halloween), you hear "Trick or treat!" and three candy
apples fall a few steps in front of you, sparkling where they land. They're all yours: only you can
see them and pick them up. Indoors they pop up from the floor instead.

- **Eat one** for a treat: a Candy Apple makes you speedy or bouncy for a little while. On Bedrock,
  if you're full, hold it and tap the ground.
- **A Golden Candy Apple** is very rare and gives 5 tokens (up to 10 a day). Your first shower
  always has one.
- **Find 31** and you get the **Trick-or-Treat Bag 2026**, yours for good. Put your candy apples in
  it! A yellow bar shows how many you've found. Bag full? Walk over your candy anyway: it still
  counts.
- Type `/hcm candy` to see how many you've found and when the next shower comes.

Candy apples don't fall in the Games world, during a game, or when you've stood still for 5
minutes.

## Minis

1. **Open a Card Pack.** Buy one on the PC's Card Packs site or in the Arcade, then right-click it. You get **one Card**.
2. **A Card** says which Mini it makes and how rare it is.
3. **Take it to a Printer** with some **filament** (craft it or buy it) and print it.
4. **Your Mini!** Every Mini is numbered, and some come out Shiny.
5. **Show it off** in a Display Case, or see every Mini there is in the **Museum**.

Each pack shows its chances for every rarity, and how many of its Minis you're still missing.
Click a pack to see every Mini inside it, marked ✓ (you have it), missing, or sold out.

Your Display Case, Vending Machine and Pallet are yours: only you (or an admin) can break one. When
one is broken, everything in it goes back to your **Mailbox** (at a Mailbox or on the PC), never
on the ground.

## Wild Minis

Sometimes a Mini appears in the wild near someone. Everyone hears about it in chat, and whoever
reaches it first keeps it.

1. **Everyone hears:** "A wild Mini appeared near …!"
2. **Hints** follow as time goes by: first the biome, then which way to go.
3. **A light beam** shows where it is near the end.
4. **Catch it!** Right-click it to keep it. Be quick: after about 5 minutes it gets away.

Two things help:

- **Mini Radar:** use it while a wild Mini is out. It tells you Cold, Warm, Hot… Burning!
- **Mini Lure:** set it, and the next wild Mini appears near you.

The Arcade shows if a wild Mini is out right now, who it's near and how long is left.

## Games

Type `/hcm play`, or click **Play** in the Arcade, to see every game. They're there once the
server turns them on.

| Game | What you do | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| **Ore Slots** | Spin three reels of ores. Match them to win. | Put in 1, 2 or 5 |
| **Twenty-One** | Get closer to 21 than the Arcade, without going over. | Put in 5, 10 or 20 |
| **The Wheel** | Spin 24 spaces. Each one shows what it gives you. | Put in 5, 10 or 20 |
| **Higher or Lower** | Is the next card higher or lower? Stop when you like. | Put in 10, 20 or 50 |
| **Coin Flip** | You and a friend put in the same. One flip, one winner. | Put in 5, 10 or 25 |
| **Cabinets** | Creeper Sweeper, Ore Merge, Snake, Mini Match, Simon Says, Whack-a-Zombie, Connect Four and Tic-Tac-Toe. | Free |
| **Time trials** | Race a parkour, elytra or boat course through every checkpoint. Beat the clock! | Free |
| **Mini golf** | Putt the ball into the cup in as few strokes as you can. A golf ball you bought, or one of your Minis, is the ball! | Free |

### Games of chance give back less than you put in

Before you play, each game of chance tells you how much it gives back, like "gives back about 89
of every 100 tokens". The games above give back about 90 of every 100 tokens you put in (the
Scratch Ticket about 89). So if you put 100 tokens into one of them, a little at a time, you get
about 90 back. Some days you get more, some days less, but over lots of plays the game keeps
about 10. Play them for fun, not to get more tokens.

- Every result is picked before the reels move. Closing the screen doesn't change it.
- Each game has a few plays a day, and there's a limit on the tokens you can put into all of them
  in a day.
- If you leave in the middle of a card game, it's finished for you, fairly.

### Take a break

Click the **blue bed** in your Wallet, or type `/hcm play break`.

- **Set a daily limit:** the most tokens you want to put into games of chance each day.
- **Pause** games of chance for 1, 7 or 30 days.

A lower limit starts right away. A higher one waits 7 days, so you can't change your mind in a
hurry. A pause can't be made shorter. It covers Crates, Scratch Tickets and Card Packs bought with
tokens too.

### Skill games pay for your time

The cabinets, time trials and mini golf are free, and they pay tokens for every first finish, goal
and medal: about a token for every minute you spend on something new.

- **Milestones:** bronze, silver and gold. Each pays once, ever, and all at once: if today's limit
  can't pay all of a medal, it waits for another day.
- **Today's challenge:** the same board for everyone. Your first try of the day counts. In
  Creeper Sweeper the clock starts as soon as today's board appears. If you're somewhere tokens
  can't be earned (like creative mode), today's board is just practice, and your scored try waits
  for later.
- **Today's pick:** a bonus the first time you finish it each day.
- **Courses and golf:** your first finish of each course, the best time of the week, the course
  of the week, a round of golf at par or better, and a hole-in-one.

You can win up to 60 tokens a day from all skill games together (the first time you finish a
course pays extra). After that, your scores still count. Beating your own best doesn't pay, but
it goes on the high scores.

### The Clubhouse

Courses and golf start and end in the Clubhouse, the room where everyone waits and hangs out.
Every invite there (a race, golf together, a game against a friend, a ride, a challenge) opens a
small **Play?** screen with Yes and Not now, so the youngest can say yes without clicking chat or
typing; it opens once per invite, only when nothing else is open, and never in the middle of a
run. **Challenge** in the Clubhouse kit (or Challenge someone on a course's screen) races anyone
who's free: it is a race party of two, so the party screen's Start and the trip back work as
they always did. Set a course's record and come back: NEW RECORD! for the whole room and a
firework over the podium, and the record goes in the trophy case on the wall. On a run by
yourself the checkpoints show how you're doing against your own best on that course, and the
finish says where you gained or lost. The jukebox in the corner plays a record for the room.
**Family week** gives points for playing (a run, a best, a record, a win, a round, a cheer: at
most 10 a day) on the Fame board and My stats; the week turns at midnight on the weekly quests'
day (Monday unless the owner changed it), not at 4 in the morning like the Weekly Cup, and when it
turns, last week's top three get their Photo time on the podium. Points are never tokens.

## The Copper Golem Brush

A Copper Golem is a little sorter: it takes a stack out of a copper chest and walks it to a chest
that already holds the same thing. Vanilla lets it pick any chest it finds. The brush lets you pick
for it. Craft it from three feathers, a slime ball and any copper block. Tap the golem, tap its
chests, tap the golem again; the golem's name tag counts them and a number floats over each one
while you hold the brush. After that the golem still takes from any copper chest but only ever
puts things into its own chests, and if none of them is in reach or has room it just stands there
holding the item and its tag says so. The golem only looks about 32 blocks around where it walks
and one block up or two down, so keep its chests close to its copper chest. Two shift-taps on the
golem clear the list; one tap never does, so nobody loses a setup by accident. Chests you pick are
also marked for CraftBridge, so a workbench pull never empties the slot the golem sorts by.
